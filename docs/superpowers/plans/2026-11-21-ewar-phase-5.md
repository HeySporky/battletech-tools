# E-War Phase 5 (Alpha Strike E-War Specials) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Alpha Strike roster play applies its E-War specials instead of only listing them: ECM bubbles and ECCM, probes, stealth and mimetic armor, AMS/RAMS, Narc beacons, TAG, C3 networks, drones, jammers. The Classic-to-Alpha Strike conversion also gets the specials the ASC v1.6 errata and the MUL give.

**Architecture:** A new rules module, `src/utils/alpha-strike-ewar-play.ts`, sits beside the Classic `ewar-play.ts` (the spec calls for its own module). It is pure and works on a small `IASEWarUnit` interface. Each unit carries optional state (`AlphaStrikeUnit.ewarPlay`). A force board (`AlphaStrikeForce.ewarBoard`) holds friendly ECM cover, C3 networks and drone control. The edition table (`IAlphaStrikeEdition.ewarRules`) gains numbers and flags. One new view file, `src/ui/pages/alpha-strike/roster/_asEWar.tsx`, holds the per-card block and the board panel. The SVG card is not changed.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, React 18, Vitest (PowerShell only).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-20-ewar-play-design.md`: section "Alpha Strike", Appendix A "Alpha Strike (Task 12)" with the ASC v1.6 conversion errata, Calls 1-8 of the design. Earlier plans: `2026-11-20-ewar-phase-0.md` to `2026-11-21-ewar-phase-4.md`. Issue #642 (closes after this phase).

## Global Constraints

- Every Alpha Strike edition the app has (memory every-edition-every-rule.md). Play follows `DEFAULT_ALPHA_STRIKE_EDITION` ("alpha-strike-commanders-edition"). The 2013 book is not selectable (`complete` is unset), but every module function takes the edition tag, so the 2013 book plays by its own entries once it is entered whole. A special the 2013 book prints and the Commander's Edition drops stays available there as apocryphal, playing by the 2013 entry (spec "Alpha Strike"; none do today, and the lookup must still do it).
- Edition differences read from the extracts (ResourceLibrary `text/AlphaStrike-CGL-2015-Corrected2ndPrinting.txt` and `text/Alpha-Strike-Commander-s-Edition.txt`; both text layers are clean, so no page pull is expected): only ASCE prints the STL own-ECM paragraph and the End Phase toggle (ASCE p.79; 2013 p.48 stops after the battle armor line), and only ASCE burns out the C3 slave when an emergency master shuts down (ASCE p.82; 2013 p.51 lacks it). RAMS, NC3, NOVA, JAM, DJ, HJ and TSEMP are in ASCE only.
- Numbers (ASCE, and 2013 where it prints the special): ECM radius 12", LECM 2", AECM counts as 2 suites, WAT = ECM + LPRB, NOVA = ECM + PRB + a 3-unit C3i that only a hostile NOVA disrupts. PRB 18", LPRB 12", BH 26" (only AECM beats it). STL: non-infantry +1 Medium, +2 Long and Extreme; battle armor +1 Short and Medium, +2 Long and Extreme. MAS +3 and LMAS +2 to non-physical attacks when the unit stood still. AMS -1 damage (minimum 1) from IF, SRM and LRM attacks, front arc only unless the AMS is in a turret. RAMS: the AMS for itself, or -1 to a friendly unit within 2", not both in one turn. Narc/iNarc: +1 damage from IF, LRM and SRM attacks for the rest of the game, unless the target is inside a friendly ECM bubble. Networks: C3 four units, C3i six, C3BS as C3 (only AECM affects it), NC3 six large craft and -1 to-hit in aerospace combat (only JAM stops it). C3EM runs 2 turns after the End Phase it activates in. C3RS: 4 sensors a game, each a stationary C3S for one turn. DJ/HJ: 34", 5 turns, 9+ on 2D6 resists. HJ stops TAG, all C3 and NOVA, and gives +1 to attacks that include IATM, LRM, CNARC, SNARC or SRM. DJ stops AECM, ECM, LECM, STL and WAT. MHQ 7 or higher may stand in for one ECM/ECCM field.
- ECM/ECCM (ASCE p.161, 2013 p.91): ECM, AECM and WAT may switch to ECCM in the End Phase. An Angel is two fields, each set separately. An Angel set to split acts only as a standard ECM, and one set to two ECCM fields acts as no ECM.
- No hex map: coverage, line of sight and range are declared by the player (design Call 1). Every modifier shows a label with its value and book page (design anti-goal).
- **Our rules only at levels 6 and 7** (NGCFells, 2026-11-21). Canon is 0-4, Apocryphal 5, Custom Homebrew 6, Munchkin 7. Every Alpha Strike special or rule that is ours, rather than a book's, is present only when the unit's rules level is 6 or higher. That covers `ESpecialAbilityType.Custom` codes such as CHF#, our conversions such as the Personal ECM's LECM, and the play effects of either. Conversion already gates codes (`allowsAlphaStrikeAbilityCode(code, rulesLevel)` in `equipment-registry.ts`, and the 'Mech card's own `rulesLevel` is raised to 6 when it carries one). The module must gate effects the same way: `getASEWarSpecials` drops a Custom code when the unit's `rulesLevel` is below 6 (test: a CHF2 card at level 4 shows and plays nothing).
- Phase 4 Calls 1-4 were confirmed by NGCFells on 2026-11-21. The force's edition (`AlphaStrikeForce.rulesEdition`, Task 5) is what the module reads, so the Alpha Strike edition selector (#646) can plug in without touching this phase's code.
- Saves: `ewarPlay` and `ewarBoard` are optional. Old saves load unchanged, and a unit with no E-War specials shows the same card numbers and blocks as before.
- Condense and extend: the one new source file is the module, plus one view file (`_asEWar.tsx`). Module tests go in `src/utils/alpha-strike-ewar-play.test.ts`. Class tests join `alpha-strike-unit.test.ts` and `alpha-strike-force.test.ts`. Data tests join `src/data/alpha-strike-special-abilities.test.ts`.
- Check the ResourceLibrary extract FIRST. Pull a page image only if the extract is missing, garbled or in doubt. Every page image pulled goes back WHOLE into every printing's extract and `_corrected_pages.md`, pushed.
- Vitest: `npx vitest run --project unit <path>` in PowerShell. `npm run check` before deploy. Bash heredocs with an apostrophe are blocked: write scripts with Write.
- Commits end with the session model's `Co-Authored-By` line.

## Calls (recorded in Appendix A and the news bullet; NGCFells's answers of 2026-11-21 in brackets)

1. **ECM against ECCM.** [Confirmed.] Hostile ECM-mode suites covering a unit are set against friendly ECCM-mode suites covering it. The higher count wins and a tie cancels both (the p.161 example: 1 ECM is cancelled by 1 ECCM, 2 ECM beat 1 ECCM). Friendly ECM cover against hostile ECCM is worked out the same way. A hostile Angel keeps its Angel-only effects (it beats BH and C3BS) only when both its fields are ECM and the hostile side wins.
2. **STL jams its own unit, by edition.** [Confirmed: "do it by edition", with #646.] Under the Commander's Edition (`stealthJamsOwnEcm`, ASCE p.79), a non-infantry unit with STL switched on counts as inside hostile ECM for its own probes, C3 and drones, and friendly ECCM does not clear that. Its own ECM projects nothing, except that an AECM may keep one ECCM field. Under the 2013 book (p.48 has no such paragraph) none of this applies, and there is no End Phase toggle either. The rule is read from the force's edition (`AlphaStrikeForce.rulesEdition`), never from a hard-coded book.
3. **Damage order.** [Confirmed.] On an IF/SRM/LRM hit the Narc +1 is added first, then AMS or RAMS takes 1 off, with a minimum of 1.
4. **Conventional infantry and stealth.** [Revised after NGCFells asked why the battle armor line; confirmed 2026-11-21.] The book never gives conventional infantry STL. The STL entry (ASCE p.79, 2013 p.48) covers non-infantry and battle armor targets only. The MUL has no CI unit with STL: its six stealthy CI units (Assassin, Tau Wraith, Sniper, DEST Heavy Jump Infantry, Commando, SpecOps Paratrooper) all carry LMAS. The Companion converts dermal camouflage to LMAS (ASC p.127, ASCE p.140). So:
   - (a) Our infantry converter, which today gives sneak suits, DEST suits and dermal camouflage no special at all, gives them LMAS as the MUL does (Task 1).
   - (b) A CI card that lists STL anyway (typed in by hand) gets no modifier. Its line reads "STL on conventional infantry: no printed modifier (ASCE p.79 covers non-infantry and battle armor)".
5. **TSEMP.** [NGCFells: build the effect in if possible. It is, so it is built (Task 4).] It covers the attacker's uses per turn (or per scenario for `-O` one-shots), Short and Medium range only, and the target effect roll:
   - −1 against a BattleMech or aerospace unit, −2 against LG, +2 against a support vehicle.
   - No effect on conventional infantry, DropShips, VLG or SLG.
   - 8+ shuts the target down for 1 turn; 7 or less gives +1 to its attacks and Control Rolls for 1 turn.
   - Repeat hits do not stack, and the effect wears off in the End Phase of the following turn (ASCE p.90; ASCE only).
   - The same table applies when our own unit is hit.
6. **The custom "Personal" ECM converts to LECM.** [NGCFells: create a conversion, put it in the entry and the TRO.] Its own hex is LECM's 2-inch radius (one hex is 2 inches), and LECM has no ECCM mode, which ASCE p.161 gives only to ECM, AECM and WAT. That matches "a Guardian for its own hex, no ECCM mode". The v1.6 errata limit LECM to battle armor for canon gear; this conversion is ours, for Custom Homebrew (Task 1).

## Review Focus

1. An old save with no `ewarPlay` or `ewarBoard`, or a unit with no E-War specials: card to-hit numbers, damage and End Round behave exactly as before (Tasks 5, 6).
2. A board naming a unit that left the force or was reset (ECM cover, C3 members, drone control) is skipped with no crash, and the save drops it on export (Tasks 3, 5).
3. A wrecked carrier projects no ECM and drops out of every network. A wrecked master takes its network down and activates the C3EM in the End Phase (Tasks 2, 4).
4. MUL spellings parse: `C3M 2`, `C3BSM2`, `RSD 1`, `TSEMP-O 1`, the turret fragments `TUR(2/2/1` ... `AMS)` (the AMS counts as turreted), and `ECM` never matches inside `AECM` or `LECM` (Task 2).
5. Damage panel edges: AMS on a 1-point hit stays 1; Narc plus friendly ECM gives no +1; a rear-arc hit with no turreted AMS gets no reduction; RAMS used for a friend cannot also defend itself that turn (Task 3).
