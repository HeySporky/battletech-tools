# E-War Phase 4 (Blue Saviour Hooks and Personal ECM Cover) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The Pleiades Blue Saviour's two waiting clauses play for real (missiles treated as met by an anti-missile system, the unit inside a hostile ECM field it cannot negate with ECCM), and the "Personal" ECM Suite covers the other friendly units that share its hex.

**Architecture:** Extend `src/utils/ewar-play.ts` (carrier hook `isBlueSaviourOn?()`, which `BattleMech` already has, so no class change), the force board (`IEWarBoard.personalEcm`) and the two views that show it (`roster/play.tsx` take-damage dialog, `components/ewar-notes.tsx`). No new source file. The reminder lines in `src/utils/future-wars-play.ts` shrink to what is not live yet.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, React 18, Vitest (PowerShell only).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-20-ewar-play-design.md` (Phase 4 line, Calls 1-5, Appendix A "ECM, ECCM and probes" row `ecm-personal`) and `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-20-pleiades-mechworks-design.md` (Blue Saviour "Post gives", Personal ECM). Earlier plans: `2026-11-20-ewar-phase-0.md` to `2026-11-21-ewar-phase-3.md`. Issue #642 (stays open for Phase 5).

## Global Constraints

- Every rules edition, every unit type (memory every-edition-every-rule.md). The unit's own edition governs its defenses and its own attack ticks; an edition with no `ewarPlayRules` gets no E-War effect (the Blue Saviour keeps its two reminder lines there). The Blue Saviour and the Personal ECM are Custom Homebrew at the default edition (Total Warfare), apocryphal and playing by the same entry in the BattleMech Manual and the Core Rulebook (existing records; do not change them).
- Condense and extend: no new source file; tests join `src/utils/ewar-play.test.ts`, `src/classes/battlemech-best-of-future-wars-play.test.ts`, `src/classes/battlemech-force.test.ts` and `roster/play.test.tsx` / `components/ewar-notes.test.tsx`.
- Calls made here (recorded in the spec's Appendix A and the news bullet; all four confirmed by NGCFells 2026-11-21, Call 1 as written: a unit with a real AMS gains the field as an extra engagement):
  1. The field is treated as ONE anti-missile system by the unit's edition (`rules.items.ams.defense`: Total Warfare -4 cluster shift, older editions missiles destroyed), engaging one missile attack a turn (`maxPerTurn`), costing no ammunition and no heat of its own (the field already makes 7 heat). A real AMS, Laser AMS or APDS with a free engagement is used first; the field answers the next attack. It never makes the unit immune to the Arrow IV / Thunder / Swarm / Flare exclusions (`amsImmune` applies to it too).
  2. "Inside a hostile ECM field it cannot negate with ECCM": the unit counts as inside hostile ECM for its own attacks (cancels its Artemis, Narc, Apollo, C3 and so on as the edition says), for its C3 link, and friendly ECCM never cancels it. It is at least one hostile suite, not one more on top of a bubble the player ticked.
  3. Stealth off and cannot-be-hidden stay reminder lines (no stealth effect is computed on the field's own unit).
  4. The Personal ECM covers its own hex only. Friendly units the player names as sharing its hex count as inside friendly ECM (no ECCM mode, no ghost targets, not the ECM Stealth Armor needs). An enemy Personal ECM needs nothing new: the player counts it as one hostile suite when the attacker, the target or a hex on the line is its hex.
- Saves: new state optional; old saves load unchanged.
- Check the ResourceLibrary extract FIRST, pull a page image only if the extract is missing, garbled or in doubt; every page image pulled goes back WHOLE into every printing's extract and `_corrected_pages.md`, pushed (this phase is custom rules: TW p.129 AMS and TW p.134 ECM are already in the tables; no page pull expected).
- Vitest: `npx vitest run --project unit <path>` in PowerShell. `npm run check` before deploy. Bash heredocs with an apostrophe are blocked: write scripts with Write.
- Commits end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Sonnet 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.

## Review Focus

1. Blue Saviour switched off (or failed, or exploded): no AMS, no ECM cover, in every view (Tasks 1-2).
2. Blue Saviour on a unit with a real AMS: the real one is used first, the field second, never both on one attack (Task 1).
3. Blue Saviour in an edition with no E-War rules, or whose AMS rule is the older missiles-destroyed kind: no crash, the reminder lines stay where nothing is live (Tasks 1, 2).
4. A Personal ECM that is destroyed, switched off, or shut down by its own unit's stealth system covers nobody; a named hex-mate that leaves the force drops out of the saved board (Task 3).
5. A Blue Saviour unit that is also the Master or an Emergency Master of a C3 network: cut off (TW), not counted for Emergency turns (Task 2).
