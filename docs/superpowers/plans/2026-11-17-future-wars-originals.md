# Future Wars: the Remaining Work, Re-planned Against the Original Issues (#172)

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan. The owner's
> execution method is **one chunk per session**, each ending with a commit, a ledger entry and a handoff. Steps use
> checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Why this plan exists.** `2026-11-13-best-of-future-wars.md` was written from *The Best of Future Wars, Volume One*
(1991) alone. Every *Future Wars* issue is in the library, and the reprint is not always faithful: it rewrote the
economics article's numbers, dropped the Power Armor sample suits, and says nothing of the follow-up and rival
articles. This plan **replaces Chunks F to L of that plan** and adds Chunk R (re-check what is already built).
Chunks A to E of that plan are done and stand. The batch plan's Chunk 7 is unchanged and is listed here for order.

**Sources.** Originals: `JBTIICRework/magazines/Future Wars/future_wars_NN_Qyhm8x.pdf` (issues 1-25, 27, 30, 31,
38-41) and `Future Wars Magazine Issue 0NN - Future Wars Magazine.pdf` (26, 28, 29, 32-37). Extracts:
`ResourceLibrary/text/future-wars-NN-Qyhm8x.*` and `Future-Wars-Magazine-Issue-0NN-...`. **Issues 1-10 have only a
garbled text layer: read them from page images** (render at 100-110 dpi). Issues 11 onward OCR well enough to find
things; every number still comes from the page image (memory: OCR, correct from PNG). PDF page and printed page
differ by issue: check the folio on the image.

## Global constraints

The Best-of plan's Global Constraints apply, with one change to the credit: cite the **original** issue and page
first and the Best-of reprint second, where both exist: "<Author>, '<Title>', *Future Wars* #N (year), p.N; reprinted
in *The Best of Future Wars, Volume One* (1991), p.N". Where the two disagree, the original wins unless the owner
rules otherwise, and the notes say what the reprint changed.
