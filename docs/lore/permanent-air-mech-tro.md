# Technical Readout: Permanent Air 'Mech

A Land-Air 'Mech with only one mode: it is always an AirMech and never converts. Fan-made, NOT OFFICIAL, offered at the
Custom Homebrew rules level. The idea is Red Pins'; the designs are LAMFAN's; the full rules were never published, so
the rules in this app are NGCFells's extrapolation from the Land-Air 'Mech rules, Inspired by both. The in-character
history below is NGCFells's too, built on LAMFAN's dates; where it says more than he does, the fault lies with NGCFells.

## History

The Land-Air 'Mech was a Star League marvel, and the Succession Wars made it a curse. Its conversion equipment could not
be made again once the factories fell, and every LAM in a lance was one bad landing from becoming a museum piece.
Militia engineers who could not keep a LAM converting found that they could keep one flying: lock it in AirMech form,
strip out the conversion gear, and what was left was a 'Mech that hopped and glided over any ground and needed
nothing it could not build.

The Logan Prime Militia made the most of it. Its Sparrow of 2844 is an unarmed scout and racer that fights with the
weapons in its hands; the Magpie of 2848 became the militia's main engagement and intercept 'Mech, in the SRM-armed 1E
and the faster, laser-armed 3A; and by 2867 the 50-ton Dire Hawk carried the idea into the medium class, as a raider in
the 4K and a bombardment 'Mech, with four rocket launchers, in the 3D. The militia's pilots called them Air-Slip Mechs.
Engineers elsewhere called them Permanent Air 'Mechs, and the name in this readout is theirs.

## What Sets One Apart

- **One mode.** It is always in AirMech mode and never converts, so it carries no conversion equipment: no weight, no
  cost.
- **Wings.** Its wings weigh the Inner Sphere Partial Wing's 7% of its mass, rounded up to the half ton, and take no
  slots of their own.
- **No landing gear.** It keeps the LAM avionics (head and each side torso) and lands on its feet.
- **Movement.** AirMech Cruise MP is its Jump MP x 3, so it needs at least 3 Jump MP.
- **Heavy PAMs.** A Permanent Air 'Mech of 56 to 75 tons flies on Jump MP x 2 and its wings weigh 15% of its mass. It
  may hover like any AirMech.
- **Fuel.** With no conversion equipment to hold it, the LAM's ton of fuel (80 points) is a required ton of its own.
  AirMech movement on the ground map spends none; Fuel Tanks may be added for space.
- **Everything else is a LAM's:** the engine, gyro, armor, structure and single-location limits, biped only, Inner
  Sphere only, and no bomb bays, since it never takes Fighter Mode to drop them.
- **Battle Value** uses the LAM rules unchanged and is provisional.

## In This App

Pick "Permanent Air 'Mech" as the LAM type in step 1 of the 'Mech Creator at the Custom Homebrew rules level. The five
Air-Slip Mechs load from the same step, rebuilt under these rules. AirMech movement in play is not built yet
(enhancement #634). Each sample's Alpha Strike Point Value below is the app's conversion of the design, NOT OFFICIAL.

## The Sample Designs

LAMFAN's five Air-Slip Mechs, rebuilt under the rules above. Four of the five carry two fewer jump jets than his posts, to make room
for the required ton of fuel; each entry's notes say exactly what changed.
