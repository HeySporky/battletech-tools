# Technical Readout: The Best of Future Wars Equipment

Fan-made equipment from *The Best of Future Wars, Volume One* (1991), offered at the Custom Homebrew rules level. NOT OFFICIAL. Where a number is ours rather than the article's, the item's notes in the equipment list say so; cost and Battle Value left at 0 are unverified.

## EII Point Defense System

*Mike Reese, p.8.* An experimental gatling machine gun with its own radar, tied to the unit's fire control.

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Weight** | 2 tons (0.5 ton of ammunition built in, 50 bursts) |
| **Slots** | 2 |
| **Damage** | 4 |
| **Range** | 1 / 2 / 3 |
| **Extra ammunition** | 100 bursts a ton |

When SRM or LRM fire is declared against the unit, the defender rolls a die: that many SRM, or that many plus 1 LRM, are shot down. One burst per salvo; it can answer every salvo in a turn.

## Long Range Flamer

*Karl Sanders, p.28.* A fuel-fed flamer whose blaze sticks to the target.

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Weight** | 5 tons |
| **Slots** | 2 |
| **Damage** | 5, then 2, then 0 over three turns, plus 5 heat each turn |
| **Heat** | 8 |
| **Range** | 4 / 8 / 12 (+2 at medium, +3 at long) |
| **Ammunition** | 15 shots a ton |

A miss scatters and sets the hexes it strikes on fire.

## The Penitrator

*Herb Barents, p.20.* A long-range missile with a soft shaped-charge head that gets critical hits through armor rather than damaging it.

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Launcher** | 3 tons, 2 slots (slots ours, as the SRM 6) |
| **Ammunition** | 2 missiles a ton |
| **Range** | minimum 8; 12 / 24 / 36 |

Roll a critical hit check for each missile that hits. Damage falls off with range; the notes give each band.

## Gauss Rifle Shells: VHE and Inferno

*Chris Congdon, "The Development of the VHE Shell", pp.9-11.* Special shells for the Gauss Rifle: the Very High Explosive shell and the Liao Inferno shell. Both also feed the Clan Gauss Rifle in this app (our choice). The Inferno shell is not explosive.

## Autocannon SABOT Rounds

SABOT rounds for the AC/2, AC/5, AC/10 and AC/20. The shots a ton and the rules for each round are in its notes in the equipment list.
