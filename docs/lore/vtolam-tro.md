# Technical Readout: VTOLAM

A Land-Air 'Mech whose second mode is a VTOL instead of a fighter. Fan-made, NOT OFFICIAL, offered at the Custom Homebrew
rules level under two rule sets by two authors. The in-character history below is NGCFells's, Inspired by both authors'
backgrounds; where it says more than they do, the fault lies with NGCFells.

## History

The Star League never fielded a rotor 'Mech, but one of its successor realms tried. The Rim Worlds Republic drew a
Locust that folded into a VTOL in 2764, in the last years of Amaris's rule, and the plans went to ground with the
Republic. They stayed lost for nearly four centuries, until Interstellar Expeditions and a Jackson Jaguar unit
recovered them in 3144. Interstellar Expeditions sold the plans as freely as it found them, and within a few years
Clan Hell's Horses, the Lyran Commonwealth, the Federated Suns and the Draconis Combine all had VTOLAMs on test.

House Liao came to the idea by another road. After Grey Monday the Capellan Confederation began many experimental
designs for its build-up against Davion space and the Republic of the Sphere, and the Hell's Horses QuadVees showed
what a second mode could do without the old LAMs' troubles. Its answer was a Locust that converts to a VTOL: by 3150
two prototypes were on Hellespont's testing grounds, built by Bergan for the CCAF under Hellespont's eye, and showing
promise as scouts, raiders and search-and-rescue units.

> *"A fighter that can stand up is a fine thing. A helicopter that can stand up is a thing a MechWarrior can actually
> fly."* Attributed to a Hellespont test pilot, 3150.

## What Sets One Apart

A VTOLAM gives up the LAM's AirMech and fighter modes for a VTOL mode. It cannot fly in vacuum and is far slower than
a fighter, but it needs no fuel, its conversion gear is lighter on the chassis, and it lands anywhere a VTOL can.

**Under BishopSteiner's rules** the VTOLAM is "a form of Bimodal LAM": conversion equipment is 20% of its tonnage, it
weighs at most 30 tons (the VTOL limit), it needs no jump jets, and it may use any fusion engine and any gyro. It
carries no fuel and lands on retractable skids, one slot in each side torso. In VTOL mode its Cruise MP is the engine
rating plus the VTOL suspension factor, divided by its tonnage; Flank MP is half again. In 'Mech mode its rotor armor
is the rear center torso: 2 points of damage there destroy the rotors, and it can no longer convert.

**Under professorheyteen's rules** conversion equipment is 10% of its tonnage, rounded up to the half ton, with rotor
equipment in each side torso and conversion equipment in each leg. It weighs at most 35 tons and may carry anything a
BattleMech can, special engines, gyros, armor, structure and myomer included; it cannot yet be an OmniMech. In VTOL
mode its Cruise MP is its Jump MP and Flank MP half again. It takes 'Mech hit locations in both modes, but a 2 is a
rotor hit: the first adds +3 to every Piloting roll in VTOL mode, the second destroys the rotor and it crashes from
its flight level. A critical hit on the rotor or conversion equipment ends transformation. It converts while jumping
or at cruising speed, and may convert without its arms.

## In This App

Pick the LAM type in step 1 of the 'Mech Creator at the Custom Homebrew rules level: "VTOLAM" for BishopSteiner's rules,
or "VTOLAM, Battletech Into The Future rules" for professorheyteen's. The creator applies the conversion weight, the
tonnage limit and the movement; the samples load from the same step. In play mode a VTOLAM switches between 'Mech and
VTOL. The rotor rules are shown as text; apply them by hand. Each sample's Alpha Strike Point Value below is the app's
conversion of the design, NOT OFFICIAL.

## The Sample Designs

Eight designs ship with the app: BishopSteiner's Locust and professorheyteen's seven, each built at its published
weights, armor and equipment. professorheyteen's posts give no model numbers, so none are invented, and all seven of
his Battle Values match his published figures. Each entry's notes say what was added to build it.
