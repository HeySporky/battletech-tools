# Technical Readout: SeaDropships

An aerodyne DropShip with a deep, sealed hull that lands on water as on a runway, sails afloat as a ship and can still
drop its landing gear for a runway or clear ground. Fan-made, NOT OFFICIAL, offered at the Custom Homebrew rules level.
The in-character history below is NGCFells's; where it says more than Vehrec's idea does, the fault lies with NGCFells.

## History

The Terran Hegemony settled many water worlds, and its first spaceports were never enough for them. A ship that could
set down in a harbor and unload at the quay needed no port at all. The Hegemony's ocean-world marines took the first
Pelican in 2655: an assault carrier that lands on a roadstead, floods its well deck, and sends hovercraft and boats
over the stern to the beach while its 'Mechs wait inside for the quay. Merchant lines followed around 2690 with the
Petrel, an ocean freighter that loads wherever there is water deep enough, and the Navy's coastal squadrons took the
Gannet around 2710 to police the sea lanes the other two depended on.

The Hegemony was not alone. In 2721 the Capellan Confederation's Nikanor group flew StingerTheRaven's Pregnant Guppy,
a slow, bloated flying boat built first for civilian trade, whose military Bluewater model carries hovercraft in the bow
and monitors in a floodable stern.

When the Star League fell, spaceports burned and the great shipyards went with them. The SeaDropships survived on
water worlds and in the Periphery, where a ship that needs no spaceport is worth keeping flying.

## What Sets One Apart

**The Flotation Hull** weighs 5% of the ship's tonnage and multiplies the whole ship's cost by 1.25. The cost multiplier is canon: the
Flotation Hull does that for fighters and vehicles (*Tactical Operations: Advanced Units & Equipment*, p.114). The 5% weight is ours, set by NGCFells, since the canon hull weighs nothing. Only an
aerodyne DropShip may have it: a spheroid lands on its tail and has no deep hull, and keeps the canon rules, partial
submersion included. It does not make a DropShip a submarine. The weight is the sealant, bilge pumps, reinforcement and
anti-corrosion plating that keep the hull sound at sea (StingerTheRaven's 200 tons on his 4,000-ton Guppy, the same 5%).
On the BattleTech forums thread, Natasha Kerensky offered the Tactical Operations amphibious chassis weights instead
(4% Limited, 10% Fully Amphibious) and StingerTheRaven took 10% in his revision; the hull stays at 5%, since it does
not give the full amphibious movement a combat vehicle buys with 10%.

- **Landing and takeoff.** It lands and takes off horizontally on Depth 1+ water as on a runway, extending the canon
  rule for conventional fighters. A vertical water landing or liftoff follows *Strategic Operations: Advanced Aerospace
  Rules* (p.61).
- **Afloat.** It moves as a large naval vessel, Cruise 2 and Flank 3 on the ground map, on water jets. The other
  floating rules of *Strategic Operations: Advanced Aerospace Rules* (p.61) still apply: heat sinks work at x1.1,
  there is no -2 grounded to-hit, Nose and Aft critical rolls take +1, and the ship drifts under Water Flow.
- **Bays afloat.** Hover vehicles launch from the vehicle bays down a bow ramp into the water and are recovered up it
  (StingerTheRaven's). Naval vehicles leave and enter a well deck or vehicle bay as ground units leave a grounded
  DropShip (*Total Warfare* p.89, as the rules team answered on the BattleTech forums, Blue Water Naval Units and
  Vehicle Bays, 13 August 2020). In a turn the ship spends MP afloat, only units with Jumping or VTOL MP may disembark,
  as from a Large Naval Vessel (*Tactical Operations: Advanced Units & Equipment* p.25).
- **Alpha Strike.** Our special FLT (Custom Homebrew and Munchkin levels only): the unit lands on water and moves afloat
  with the n movement mode; afloat, its vehicle bays use the bow ramp, and nothing leaves it in a turn it moves.

**The Well Deck** is a flooded bay: 50 tons for the vehicle it holds plus 10% each for the gates and seals, the support equipment and the berthage, 65 tons. It holds
one hover or naval vehicle of up to 50 tons, has a stern door, and launches and recovers vehicles only while the ship
is afloat. It needs the Flotation Hull.

**The Large Well Deck** carries one Large Naval Vessel, a wet-navy ship too big for any vehicle bay, such as the
8,500-ton Rapier patrol destroyer that Chaeronea wanted to carry. It is bought by the ton at the Well Deck's own rate:
1.3 tons of bay for each ton of vessel (The_Caveman's 10% for the pumps is the Well Deck's support share; the gates and
seals and the berthage are the other two tenths) and 200 C-bills a ton. An 11,050-ton bay floats out an 8,500-ton
ship. It needs a door and the Flotation Hull, floods and drains like a drydock, and launches or recovers its vessel only
while the ship is afloat and has not moved that turn. Alpha Strike: our special NVT#-D# (Custom Homebrew and Munchkin
levels only), the vessels carried and the doors, as the other transport specials are written.

## Water Landings by Edition

| Book | What it says about water | With the Flotation Hull |
|---|---|---|
| *AeroTech* (1986); *BattleTech Manual* (1987); *BattleSpace* (1993) | Craft landing in a water hex +3. No floating rule. | The +3 is waived. |
| *DropShips & JumpShips* (1988) | No rules. | Lore only. |
| *AeroTech 2* (1998); *AeroTech 2 Revised* (2004) | Landing in water or swamp +3; a vertical landing on water leaves no crater. | The +3 is waived. |
| *Total Warfare* | +3 for a water hex; in the standard rules a unit landing in Depth 1+ water is destroyed. | A horizontal landing is a runway landing. |
| *Strategic Operations* (2011) p.73; *SO: Advanced Aerospace Rules* (2021) p.61 | Any DropShip or Small Craft may land in Depth 1+ water; an aerodyne horizontal landing crashes (x10 damage). The floating rules above. | A horizontal landing is a runway landing; afloat, Cruise 2 and Flank 3. |

A canon DropShip's water landing is never changed by any of this.

## In This App

Tick Flotation Hull in the Small Craft Creator's chassis step on an aerodyne DropShip, and add Well Deck or Large Well
Deck bays. The four ships below load from the creator's home as samples (the Pregnant Guppy is on the list too). In play mode a hulled ship tracks airborne, grounded and afloat, shows the landing
modifier of the current book and of the older books (+3, waived by the hull), sails Cruise 2 / Flank 3 afloat, marks a
turn it moved afloat, and launches and recovers well deck vehicles and bow ramp hovercraft by log. The record sheet
notes the hull, the bow ramp and each Large Well Deck's vessel tons. The Alpha Strike card shows FLT and NVT, NOT
OFFICIAL.

## The Ships

Each design is built through the creator by *TechManual* DropShip construction, so the weights, Battle Value and cost
below are read from the design itself. Each entry's notes say what the fit was for.
