# Technical Readout: Walkers

A walker is a four-legged 'Mech of 10 to 100 tons with ten locations: four legs, four outer torsos (front, left,
right and rear), a center torso and a control section that can carry a turret. It is Custom Homebrew (rules level
6), not canon, and everything this app makes for one is marked NOT OFFICIAL.

## Credits

**Walkers are the work of Clell Flint.** His rules first ran as "Rules for Building 4 Legged Mechs in BattleTech" in
*Future Wars* #11 (1989, pp. 25-27), a BattleTech fanzine, and were reprinted, with changes, as "Optional Rules for Building
Walkers" in *The Best of Future Wars, Volume One* (1991, pp. 36-38). The app offers both printings. Where they
differ (the anti-infantry to-hit number, the assault control section's slots, an immobilized walker being knocked
over), the walker follows the one it is built under.

**What is NGCFells's, and not Flint's:** the industrial walker (Inspired by Flint's rules and the IndustrialMech rules of
*TechManual*, pp. 66-71), the Battle Value, the cost, the split of an item between two locations, the whole of the Alpha Strike
rules below, and the three sample designs. Flint printed no walker designs; the art in the fanzine has no stats. If
any of it is incorrect, the fault lies with NGCFells.

The rules texts themselves are not reproduced here. They belong to their author.

## What Sets One Apart

- **Ten locations.** Four legs, four outer torsos, a center torso and a control section. The rear torso is a location
  of its own, so a walker has no rear armor on its other torsos.
- **Four legs.** A walker's Walking MP is its engine's figure times 1.25, rounded as Flint's rule says. It is steadier
  than a biped: Flint gives it a bonus on Piloting Skill Rolls.
- **Belly guns.** The center torso takes only machine guns, small lasers and flamers. They fire only at infantry and
  vehicles in the walker's own hex.
- **A turret.** The control section may carry a turret, which turns all the way round.
- **Kicks only.** A walker has no arms: it cannot punch or club, but it can kick in any direction.
- **Variant slots.** The Best of Future Wars offers a second set of slot counts, with room in the outer torsos and legs
  for twice as much. A design built under them says so.

## In This App

The Walker Creator is offered at the Custom Homebrew rules level. A design you have saved stays reachable below that
level. A design can be:

- built under either printing, standard or industrial, with its cost and Battle Value worked out step by step;
- printed on its own record sheet, with a Technical Readout of its own;
- added to a Classic BattleTech roster and played there, with damage by location, critical hits, lost legs and
  ammunition;
- printed as an Alpha Strike card, added to an Alpha Strike roster and played in an Aces campaign, where a walker
  (WK) is a BattleMech and an industrial walker (IWK) an IndustrialMech.

## Alpha Strike Rules

**NOT OFFICIAL.** No rulebook converts a walker, and Flint's rules are for the board game only. These rules are this
app's own. They follow the Alpha Strike Companion's conversion for a 'Mech (pp. 91-141, with errata v1.6 and the
Converting Heat errata v1.3) wherever it can be followed, the Companion's rules for a four-legged 'Mech (p. 40), and
Flint's play rules.

### The Card

| Stat | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Type** | WK (walker), IWK (industrial walker). Wherever Alpha Strike asks a unit's type, WK reads as BM and IWK as IM. |
| **Size** | By tonnage, as a 'Mech: under 40 tons 1, 40-55 2, 60-75 3, 80-100 4. |
| **Move** | 2 inches for each Walking MP on four legs. A jumping walker adds the j code, or prints both rates when they differ. |
| **Armor** | All ten locations' armor points, divided by 30 and rounded. |
| **Structure** | The 'Mech Structure Conversion Table by tonnage and engine, times the walker's structure points over a biped's at that tonnage, rounded; at least 1. |
| **Overheat** | As a 'Mech, by the Converting Heat errata v1.3. |
| **Damage** | As a 'Mech. The front, left and right torsos, the legs, the control section and the turret count on the damage line. The rear torso gives REAR. The center torso gives AIF#. |
| **Point Value** | As a BattleMech (an industrial walker as an IndustrialMech). WLK and AIF# add nothing. |

### Heat

A walker uses the same heat step as a 'Mech card in this app: 2 for walking (none for an industrial walker that
cannot jump) or the Jumping MP, at least 3; the forward and turret weapons' heat, or the rear torso's when it is
greater; +1 for each AMS, +5 for a Clan Laser AMS, +7 for an Inner Sphere Laser AMS. Rocket and one-shot launchers add
nothing, and neither do the center torso's belly guns. When the heat sinks dissipate less than the total less 4, the
damage is scaled down and the difference is the Overheat value, at most 4. A walker and a biped 'Mech with the same
weapons and heat sinks get the same card.

### The WLK Special Ability (Walker)

Every walker card carries **WLK**. It stands for these rules:

- **Unit type:** wherever the rules ask a unit's type, WK counts as a BattleMech (BM) and IWK as an IndustrialMech
  (IM): the 'Mech critical hit column, the Heat Scale and Overheat.
- **Four legs:** the rules for a four-legged 'Mech apply: -2 on Control Rolls to avoid skidding, -1 on bog-down
  checks, no Climbing, no External Cargo. (Flint's bonus on Piloting Skill Rolls is read as the Companion's -2.)
- **Firing arc:** the damage line is fired into the 90-degree Fore arc of a four-legged 'Mech; REAR into the rear
  arc; TUR all round.
- **Kicks only:** it may make a standard physical attack (damage equal to its Size) or a charge, and a Death From
  Above if it jumps. It has no melee weapon.
- **Belly guns:** a walker with AIF# may make that attack as well.
- **Legs:** no leg is tracked. An MP Hit on the 'Mech critical hit column stands for a lost leg.

### The AIF# Special Ability (Anti-Infantry Fire)

A walker's center-torso weapons fire only at infantry and vehicles under it. **AIF#** is the sum of their Short
values, rounded; under half a point is AIF0*.

- In addition to its other attacks, the walker may make one AIF attack on an infantry (CI, BA) or vehicle (CV, SV)
  unit in base contact, in any direction. It does # damage. It is not a physical attack and needs no firing arc.
- **To-hit:** Flint's base number, less 2, replaces the pilot's Skill. The target number is **6** under *The Best of
  Future Wars* (8 - 2) or **-2** under *Future Wars* #11 (0 - 2, which all but always hits), plus the target's Target
  Movement Modifier and the other usual modifiers; no range modifier.
- **Heat:** the attack builds no heat, and a flamer in the center torso gives no HT.

### Codes on a Walker Card

WK, IWK, WLK and AIF# are this app's own. The others are standard Alpha Strike special abilities and mean what the
Alpha Strike rulebook says they mean.

| Code | Meaning | Given by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **WK, IWK** | Unit type: walker, industrial walker | Every walker |
| **WLK** | Walker rules (above) | Every walker |
| **AIF#** | Anti-Infantry Fire (above) | Weapons in the center torso |
| **REAR#/#/#** | Rear-firing damage | Weapons in the rear torso |
| **TUR(#/#/#)** | Turret damage | Weapons in the turret |
| **OVL** | Overheat at Long range | A walker hot enough at Long range, with an Overheat value |
| **BFC, EE, FC, SEAL** | The industrial abilities | An industrial walker without Advanced Fire Control; its engine; sealing |

## The Sample Walkers

Flint printed no walker designs, so these three are NGCFells's (Custom Homebrew, NOT OFFICIAL): a light walker, a heavy
with a turret, and an assault built under the variant slots. Their figures are worked out by the app from the
designs, so they always match the creator. You can load all three from the Walker Creator's home page, under
"Sample Walkers".
