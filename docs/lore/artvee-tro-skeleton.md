# ArtVee (Mobile Artillery Platform): TRO skeleton

Custom Homebrew, not canon. This file holds the lore worked out while the ArtVee was built
(`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-20-artvee-design.md`), arranged as the outline of a Technical Readout to write up
and submit to the community. Everything under **Established** was settled during design; everything under **To write** is open.

## 1. Introduction (TRO front matter)

### Established

- Name: **ArtVee**, short for Artillery Vehicle, patterned on QuadVee. Several Inner Sphere powers call them
  **MAPs** (Mobile Artillery Platforms).
- Concept: a tracked tank that transforms the way a LAM does, between **Vehicle Mode** (it moves and fires its gun as
  an autocannon) and **Siege Mode** (stationary, the gun fires as true tube artillery).
- Design intent: strongly defended once set up, with heavy armor, protected tracks, and secondary weapons against
  infantry and fast raiders.

**To write:** the in-universe narrator and framing (who compiled the readout, and when); a short "why artillery that
moves" essay.

## 2. Technology

### Established

- **The combo gun:** one physical weapon with two fire profiles. An artillery tube (Thumper, Sniper or Long Tom) takes
  a drop-in autocannon breech and a collapsible barrel for Vehicle Mode (AC/5, AC/10 or AC/20 respectively). The
  longer barrel gives the AC profile slightly longer reach than a standard autocannon. Separate magazines feed the
  artillery and autocannon rounds.
- **Conversion equipment:** most of it in the hull, with the breech-and-barrel assembly in the turret. Destroying it
  strands the vehicle in whatever Mode it is in.
- **Transformation:** a whole turn's work. While it converts, the vehicle neither moves nor fires; the crew's only
  job is the conversion.
- **Siege Mode:** the tracks lock, the turret turns freely through 360°, and the gun fires full artillery.
- **Powerplant:** XL fusion, or XXL where the tech allows (the engine has to power everything while the tank is
  static). The nine production patterns below all use XL.
- **Armored Motive System:** recommended but not part of the original design. The Clans (Hell's Horses, 3057) and
  later the Inner Sphere (3071) fitted it.
- **Smoke:** some patterns carry the Buckler Smoke Dispenser or the Smoker (both from *Future Wars* #32) to screen the
  emplacement.

**To write:** how the conversion works mechanically (track locks, outrigger spades if any, breech swap), and the
crew's drill and timing.

## 4. Doctrine (Vehicle Mode and Siege Mode)

**Established:** Vehicle Mode moves with the line and fights as an autocannon tank; Siege Mode digs in as a
battery. The transformation turn leaves it helpless (no movement, no fire), so escorts or smoke cover it.

**To write:** battery organisation, spotter pairing, counter-battery tactics, how each faction uses it.

## 5. The nine patterns (the app's sample units)

Three guns in three patterns. Designs are production technology only (no prototype equipment), so none carries
Armored Motive.

| Gun (weight class) | SLDF / Star League pattern (IS, 2700s) | Clan pattern (Clan, kept since the Exodus) | Inner Sphere MAP pattern (IS, 3071+) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Thumper (light) | Falconet | Pangolin | Springald |
| Sniper (medium) | Saker | Archerfish | Perrier |
| Long Tom (heavy) | Culverin | Glyptodon | Tormentum |

Naming themes: SLDF names are gunpowder cannon classes, smallest to largest (falconet, saker, culverin). Clan names
are armored or spitting animals (the pangolin curls up like the tank locking down; the archerfish strikes at range).
MAP names are siege engines.
