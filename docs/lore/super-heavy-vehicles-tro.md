# Technical Readout: Super-Heavy Combat Vehicles

Combat Vehicles built past their motive type's standard weight limit: 200-ton tracked fortresses, 160-ton wheeled
carriers and WiGE, 100-ton hovercraft, 60-ton VTOLs, and warships of up to 555 tons. It is canon: Tactical Operations:
Advanced Units & Equipment (p.190) gives the rules, and the app offers them at the Advanced rules level, Inner Sphere
and Clan alike. They are Combat Vehicles only; Support Vehicles have their own size classes.

## What Sets One Apart

- **Weight.** Each motive type may go up to the Super-Heavy limit in the table below. No Hydrofoil may be built in the
  Super-Heavy class.
- **Engines.** Any engine a Combat Vehicle may use, Large engines included. The engine rating still counts the motive
  type's suspension factor, which the table carries on past the standard weights. Every Super-Heavy Combat Vehicle
  needs at least 1 Cruise MP, except a trailer.
- **Internal structure.** A non-naval Super-Heavy Combat Vehicle puts 20 percent of its weight into structure, not 10,
  rounded up to the half ton. Naval vessels keep the 10 percent. Every location, turrets included, gets the tonnage / 10
  in structure points, rounded up.
- **Six facings.** Every Super-Heavy Combat Vehicle except a VTOL splits each side in two: Front, Front-Left,
  Front-Right, Rear-Left, Rear-Right and Rear, plus any turrets, each with its own armor and structure. A Super-Heavy
  VTOL keeps Front, Left, Right, Rear and Rotor, as a standard VTOL does.
- **In play.** A Super-Heavy Combat Vehicle plays as its standard weight relatives do, with three differences. Super-Heavy
  ground vehicles roll on the Large Ground Support Vehicle Hit Location Table (Total Warfare p.206), and the
  Super-Heavy Vehicle Record Sheet tracks the facings. Non-naval ones may enter Depth 1 water at the Total Warfare
  costs (p.52). And they may carry two battle armor units at a time (Total Warfare p.227).

| Naval (Displacement) | 301-555 | tonnage / 10, rounded up to the nearest 5 | Water hexes only (Depth 2+) |

## How the App Counts It

- **Construction.** At the Advanced rules level the creator lets the tonnage run to the Super-Heavy limit, doubles the
  structure weight of a non-naval vehicle, and lays out the six facings (or the VTOL's four and a rotor). A
  MegaMek file of a Super-Heavy vehicle imports onto the same locations.
- **Battle Value and cost.** The same Combat Vehicle Battle Value and cost steps as any other Combat Vehicle (TechManual
  pp.307-309 and p.279), over every facing the vehicle has. The 55-ton Kamakiri Attack VTOL comes to 1,378, as the
  Master Unit List has it.
- **Record sheet and play.** The sheet is titled for the Super-Heavy vehicle and prints the six facings with their hit
  table; a Super-Heavy VTOL prints on the VTOL sheet and table. In play, an attack from a split side strikes the
  half nearest the attacker.
- **Alpha Strike.** Armor and structure are the totals over every location, the rotor included, as for any vehicle; the
  Kamakiri's card is Armor 4, Structure 3, as the Master Unit List prints it.

## Printing Differences

The single-volume Tactical Operations (2008 and the 2012 printing, p.378) gave Super-Heavy VTOLs the six facings too.
Tactical Operations: Advanced Units & Equipment (p.190) says "apart from VTOLs", and its Super-Heavy VTOL designs, such
as the Kamakiri, carry Front, Left, Right, Rear and Rotor armor. The app builds to Advanced Units & Equipment and keeps the older six-facing layout as an apocryphal choice in the vehicle creator ("Super-Heavy VTOL facings"), saved with the design.
