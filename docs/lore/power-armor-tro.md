# Technical Readout: Power Armor

Power Armor is a suit of 5, 10 or 15 tons, built like a small two-legged 'Mech and worn by its pilot instead of
driven from a cockpit. It is Custom Homebrew (rules level 6), not canon, and everything this app makes for one is
marked NOT OFFICIAL.

## Credits

**Power Armor is the work of Bill Swisher.** His article "Power Armor: The Poor Man's Battlemech" first ran in
*Future Wars* #14 (1989, p. 39), a BattleTech fanzine, and was reprinted in *The Best of Future Wars, Volume One*
(1991, pp. 14-16). The scan of issue #14 in NGCFells's library lacks printed pages 39-42, so the app's rules are read from
the reprint. Issue #14 also printed three sample suits on standard 'Mech record sheets (pp. 43-45), which the reprint
left out; they show where the pilot sits and how the slots are laid out.

**What is NGCFells's, and not Swisher's:** no tonnage but 5, 10 and 15, and two legs only; the waldo's place on the sheet
as the samples show it; head armor at most twice head structure at every weight; the 5-ton weapon list held to the
weapons of the article's era; shielding rounded up to the half ton; the Battle Value and cost below; and the whole of
the Alpha Strike rules, Inspired by Swisher's play rules. If any of it is incorrect, the fault lies with NGCFells.

The rules text itself is not reproduced here. It belongs to its author.

## History

House Steiner has never had light 'Mechs enough to back its infantry in the cities it must hold. In 2963 a Tharkan
research project answered with a suit of armor a soldier could wear: arms, legs, an engine and a waldo system in place
of a cockpit. The Estates-General would not let it near the lines at Hesperus II. The plant's capacity, they said,
belonged to real 'Mechs.

The idea slept until 3026, when the Lyran Commonwealth and the Federated Suns began to plan their attack on the
Capellan Confederation. Scientists of the New Avalon Institute of Science and the Commonwealth could build the suits
again by 3027, and in December of that year Defiance Industries turned a line on Hesperus II from the Zeus to Power
Armor. At thirty-eight suits a month it had built 230 by August 3028. When the Fourth Succession War opened, nearly 150
of them were fighting on Liao worlds.

Steiner commanders use them to support infantry and hold cities. Davion commanders prefer to swarm a target with them.
Both have learned to aim at a 'Mech's knees. Suits also stand guard over DropShips.

**Dates in this app:** prototype 2963 (the Tharkan project), introduced 3027 (Defiance Industries, Hesperus II), never
extinct; Inner Sphere only.

## What Sets One Apart

- **Three weights.** A suit weighs 5, 10 or 15 tons and has two legs and two arms, each with its upper and lower arm
  actuators.
- **Fewer slots.** Every location has six slots and the center torso twelve. Engine, gyro and pilot fill the center
  torso, so no weapon may go there or in the head.
- **A waldo, not a cockpit.** The pilot wears the suit. The half-ton waldo system costs 50,000 C-bills and takes two
  slots in the head and two in the center torso.
- **The 5-ton suit.** It has no gyro, a frame of its own (head 1, center torso 3, side torsos 2, arms and legs 1),
  shielding of half the engine's weight around a fusion engine, and only flamers, machine guns and small lasers. Its
  pilot needs Piloting 3 or better, or 4 at +2 to every Piloting Skill Roll.
- **Hard to hit.** Every attack on a suit is at +1. It stands one level tall, and up to fourteen suits may share a hex.
- **A pilot at risk.** Hits to the head and center torso reach the pilot. There is no ejection: the pilot leaves the
  suit by unlatching it, which takes a full turn.

## In This App

Power Armor is a Special Ruleset for the 'Mech Creator, offered at the Custom Homebrew rules level. Tick "Future
Wars: Power Armor" in step 1. It cannot be ticked with Clell Flint's construction rules. A suit can be:

- built at 5, 10 or 15 tons, with the waldo, the 5-ton frame and the weapon limit checked as you go;
- printed on a 'Mech record sheet titled POWER ARMOR, with the pilot's slots, the suit's row of the Building Effects
  Table and a short rules box;
- played in a Classic BattleTech roster: the Target dialog's Power Armor box gives the +1 to hit, the pilot rules
  are applied to the log, and the suit's panel unlatches it;
- printed as an Alpha Strike card and played in an Alpha Strike roster.

## Battle Value and Cost

Swisher gives neither, so a suit is priced by the edition's rules on the design as built, the waldo standing in for
the cockpit. Shielding adds weight, not cost: the vehicle rules cost it inside the engine.

One figure is NGCFells's. The +1 to hit a suit counts as one more point of Target Movement Modifier, which adds 0.1 to the
Defensive Factor (TechManual p. 304).

NGCFells's 5-ton IPA-4 (two small lasers, 24 points of armor, walking 6) shows the figures:

| Figure | Working | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cost | 175,300 C-bills of parts, the waldo's 50,000 among them, x 1.05 | 184,065 C-bills |
| Defensive Battle Rating | 80.5 x (1 + 3 / 10 + 0.1) | 112.7 |
| Offensive Battle Rating | 23 x 1.5 | 34.5 |
| Battle Value | 112.7 + 34.5 | 147 |

## Alpha Strike Rules

**NOT OFFICIAL.** No rulebook converts Power Armor. A suit's card is the 'Mech conversion of the Alpha Strike
Companion (pp. 91-141, with errata v1.6 and the Converting Heat errata v1.3): a BattleMech (BM) of Size 1. One special
ability carries Swisher's play rules.

### The PWA Special Ability

PWA (Power Armor) is printed on every suit's card:

- **Hard to hit:** every attack against the suit is at +1 to hit.
- **Low profile:** the suit is 1 inch (1 level) tall, as a vehicle, for line of sight.
- **Stacking:** on a hex map, up to 14 suits may share a hex, or 7 when they share it with one other unit.
- **Physical attacks:** it may make a standard physical attack or a charge only against a unit on its own level, and a
  Death From Above; it may not make a melee attack. A failed Death From Above also wounds its pilot.
- **No ejection:** the pilot cannot eject. Leaving the suit takes a full turn in which it neither moves nor attacks.
- **Point Value:** the +1 to hit is priced as +1 to the unit's movement modifier in the defense factor, as Stealth
  (STL) is.

IPA-4 converts to Move 12", Armor 1, Structure 1, damage 1/0/0, ENE and PWA, at 9 points. Without PWA it would be 7.
