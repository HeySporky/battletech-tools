# Technical Readout: Beast-Mounted Infantry

Conventional infantry that rides animals into battle. It is canon: Tactical Operations: Advanced Units & Equipment
(pp.106-108) gives the rules, and the app offers them at the Advanced rules level, Inner Sphere and Clan alike.

## What Sets One Apart

- **The creature is the motive.** The creature decides the platoon's MP and how it moves (ground, submarine or VTOL),
  its terrain restrictions, the weight it adds and the damage divisor it adds to the troopers' own. Beast-mounted
  platoons never use the mechanized infantry rules.
- **Three sizes.** A Large creature (a horse, a camel, a branth) carries one trooper, and a platoon may field twenty-one.
  A Very Large creature (an elephant, an orca) carries two and a platoon may field seven; a Monstrous creature (the
  hipposaur) carries four and a platoon may field two. Each Very Large or Monstrous creature is a squad of its own.
- **Harder to hit, harder to hurt.** Attacks against a platoon on a Very Large creature take -1 to-hit, and on a
  Monstrous one -2. The creature's damage divisor divides every hit and every damage die before the platoon's
  troopers take it, and fractions go up.
- **The creature fights too.** At range 0, some creatures add bonus damage: dice against conventional infantry, and a
  fixed number of points against everything else.
- **Support weapons by size.** A Large creature carries none; a Very Large one carries up to two and a Monstrous one up
  to three, and a creature works each weapon with half the crew, rounded up.
- **Anti-'Mech attacks by size.** A Large creature leaves them all open. A Very Large one allows no Swarm attacks (the
  Creature Size Table; the rules text on p.108 says Leg attacks, and the table is followed here), and a Monstrous one
  allows no Leg or Swarm attacks, which leaves the platoon none.
- **Buildings.** A Very Large creature adds 1 MP to enter a building hex and does 2 CF of damage to it; a Monstrous one
  adds 2 MP and does 4.
- **Water.** The submarine creatures, the orca and the hipposaur, need SCUBA gear on their riders. An orca must surface
  every 180 turns to breathe and a hipposaur every 2.

## How the App Counts It

- **Weight.** Transport weight is the weight of the creatures plus 0.2 tons for each trooper, rounded up to the half
  ton. The platoon is broken down no lower than one creature and its riders.
- **Battle Value.** The creature's damage divisor is counted with the armor's before it is multiplied by 1.5 and by the
  troopers; a Very Large creature takes 0.1 off the Defensive Factor and a Monstrous one 0.2 (TO:AUE p.191).
- **Cost.** 5,000 C-bills for each ton of the whole unit, added to the troopers' cost (TO:AUE p.225).
- **Alpha Strike.** Ground creatures move as foot infantry and VTOL creatures as mechanized VTOL; the Alpha Strike
  Companion has no code for the submarine ones, so MegaMek's "s" is used. The damage divisor sets the armor as it does
  for any infantry. The bonus damage and the to-hit modifier are not converted: no book gives a rule for them.
- **Open points.** TO:AUE says the Battle Value divisors are "summed"; the app multiplies the armor's divisor by the
  creature's, as it does in play and as MegaMek does, since a creature with no divisor (1.0) would otherwise raise the
  armor's.
