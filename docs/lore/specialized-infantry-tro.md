# Technical Readout: Specialized Infantry

Conventional infantry trained and kitted for one job. It is canon: Tactical Operations: Advanced Units & Equipment
(pp.135-136, 152-153 and 162-163) gives the rules, and the app offers them at the Advanced rules level, Inner Sphere and
Clan alike. This page covers three families the creator builds on the platoon: the specialties, the SCUBA and VTOL
motive types, and the Xenoplanetary Condition-Trained (XCT) troops.

## What Sets One Apart

- **Specialists are Veteran or better.** Gunnery 3, and Anti-'Mech 4 where the platoon has the kit. One platoon may
  carry several specialties; for every limit the most restrictive of the motive type and the specialties applies.
- **Combat Engineers.** A squad of up to 10 in a platoon of up to 20 and 2 squads, with no secondary weapons. The
  platoon is trained in any of six tasks, and one is designated before the scenario: Bridge-Building, Demolition,
  Firefighting, Minesweeping, Sensor or Trench/Fieldworks. The play panel keeps each task's counters.
- **Marines** fight on in vacuum, zero-G and boarding actions as foot infantry would in normal conditions.
- **Mountain Troops** and **Paratroops** are foot infantry with 1 Ground MP and one secondary weapon a squad. Mountain
  Troops shrug off Thin atmospheres and climb 3 levels a hex; Paratroops drop from aerospace units in the atmosphere,
  and a squad that rolls 3 or less on landing loses a trooper and scatters.
- **Paramedics** and **TAG troops** are not platoons of their own: up to 2 a squad together, each replacing a
  secondary weapon trooper. A paramedic carries no weapon and does no damage and tends up to 5 wounded; a TAG trooper
  makes TAG attacks at 3/6/9 hexes, but TAG only works with at least 2 a squad. Neither slows the platoon.
- **SCUBA.** Standard SCUBA infantry walk as foot and swim at 1 MP; Motorized SCUBA walk at 1 MP and swim at 2; the
  Mechanized type uses the submarine vehicle rules at 3 MP. Weapons have half their range underwater, and none use
  disposable weapons.
- **Mechanized VTOL.** Microlite platoons (2 troopers a squad, no support weapons, 6 VTOL MP, at least 1 hex of
  movement a turn to stay up) and Micro-copter platoons (4 troopers a squad, one support weapon at most, 5 VTOL MP and a
  hover at no cost, 1 MP less with a support weapon). Both are at most 4 squads, never broken below a squad, may walk
  1 MP, and make no Anti-'Mech attacks.
- **XCT troops.** One environment (cold, hot, hazardous liquids, vacuum or trace, toxic, tainted) and an armor kit
  that suits it. They ignore extreme-temperature movement limits, divide hazardous liquid damage by 3, and take double
  damage from the suit breaches of a bad atmosphere. Troops built for one environment may not be deployed in another
  hazardous one.

## How the App Counts It

- **Weight.** The trooper weight of the motive type plus the specialty's added tons: Combat Engineers 0.1, Paratroops
  0.05, SCUBA 0.05, 0.1 and 0.9, and 0.05 for each paramedic. The book's table presumes a base of 0.1 t, which is the
  foot trooper (0.085 t) with its Anti-'Mech kit (0.015 t); the app adds its added tons to the platoon's own weight, so a
  motorized marine combat engineer with the kit is 0.21 + 0 + 0.1 = 0.31 t, as the book works it. The Microlite and
  Micro-copter weigh 1.5 and 2 t a trooper (p.152), which is the 0.1 t foot trooper plus the 1.4 and 1.9 t of the
  Construction Data (p.225).
- **Battle Value.** The unit type modifier starts at 1 and adds 0.1 for Combat Engineers, 0.3 for Marines, 0.2 for
  Mountain Troops, 0.1 for Paratroops, 0.1 for SCUBA and 0.1 for XCT troops (p.193); the sum multiplies the Defensive
  and Offensive Battle Rating before rounding. A mechanized VTOL platoon is counted as jumping for its Target Movement
  Modifier, as MegaMek counts it (the book is silent).
- **Cost.** The motive type's multiplier (Microlite x4, Micro-copter x4.5, SCUBA x2 and x2.5), then the specialties'
  (Combat Engineers x5, Marines x3, Mountain Troops x2, Paratroops x3, XCT x1.5; p.225). Each paramedic adds 0.375 of
  the finished total, last. The Construction Data has no Mechanized SCUBA or TAG troops row: the mechanized multiplier
  of 3.2 and a secondary weapon trooper with a 50,000 C-bill TAG price them, both marked provisional.
- **Alpha Strike.** Mountain Troops get MTN, Paratroops PAR, TAG troops TAG, and engineers trained for Trench/Fieldworks,
  Minesweeping or Firefighting get TRN, MSW or FF, by the Companion's conversion text. A SCUBA platoon lists its ground
  and its water Move (2"f/4"s) with the UMU special. Mechanized VTOL platoons move at "v". No book converts the other
  tasks, Marines, Paramedics or XCT troops, so they add nothing.
- **Open points.** The book's "Cooling Suit (any type)" is read as both MechWarrior cooling rows. MegaMek's files give
  no count of paramedics or TAG troops where they hold only the flag (one and two a squad are taken). The Paramedic
  weight of 0.05 t is added to the medics only; MegaMek adds it to every trooper.
